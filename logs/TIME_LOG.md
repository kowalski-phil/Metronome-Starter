# TIME_LOG — planned vs actual time, per stage, per video

The record for the time budget. Rules:
- **No log, no number.** Time exists only here. Never estimated, never filled in later.
- **No reconstructing from memory.** A stage is either logged or marked `UNLOGGED` for good.
- **An honest OVER row is a good row; a fudged row kills the log.**
- Budget: **[3.0] attended hours per video is a SOFT TARGET, not pass/fail.** Every minute still gets logged. A video over target needs a **one-line reason** in Notes (a chosen investment, not unnoticed creep). The real test is a cost/benefit review at mid-run and at the end.
- "Attended" = any minute you are actively scripting, recording, approving, reviewing, or fixing something. Unattended machine time (renders) goes in the **Machine** column and isn't counted.
- **Cut review** = watching the whole rough cut once. It takes about the video's runtime, so it grows with length.
- *Lesson from the original run:* log **build time** (making the demo) too, in its own column. It went unlogged there, and the "2.5 h per video" figure doesn't include it.
