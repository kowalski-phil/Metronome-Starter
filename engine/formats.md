# FORMAT MENU (every format can be recorded in one take)

**Output spec (example from the original run; adjust to yours):** 1920×1080 · 24 fps · H.264 · audio normalised to −14 LUFS · **separate `.srt` captions (not burned in)** · one brand accent colour and two fonts. Runtime: **8 min+** (YouTube's mid-roll ad threshold), with a sweet spot of 10–15. Never pad to reach it.

## Screen walkthrough (the default)
"Here's the thing I built. Let me walk you through what each step does and why."
- **Record:** screen + webcam + mic at the same time, one file per beat. The screen is the star. Webcam as a small bubble on teaching beats.
- **Structure:** Hook (full-screen you → title card) → 4–8 teaching beats (full screen + bubble) → Outro with a call to action (full-screen you + subscribe ask + "coming up").
- The demo is **pre-built**. On camera you explain it step by step; you don't assemble it live.
- Cheapest to record, most authentic, most forgiving of flubs.

## Talking head + slides
For judgment topics ("what broke when we automated X", "7 mistakes I'd avoid").
- More full-screen you, plus a few slides or fresh screen grabs as proof. Less live clicking.
- Same hook and outro structure.

## Emergency: "One problem, one fix"
Insurance for a week that's falling apart. It's an honourable move, never a failure.
- **≤ 90 min total production**, 5–8 min runtime. Screen and voice only: no webcam, no graphics. Cut + captions only.
- Keep **2 topic-bank entries ready** for this at all times.

*How to choose: the screen walkthrough by default. Talking head when the topic is more judgment than clicks. Emergency when the week is collapsing.*
