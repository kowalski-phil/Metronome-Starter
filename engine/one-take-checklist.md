# ONE-TAKE CHECKLIST (pre-record gate · ≤ 10 items)

Run every item before you hit record. **One continuous sitting: all beats and retakes in this session. No coming back another day to re-shoot.**

1. **Notes where you can glance at them**: the beat list on a second screen or printed, so you never stop to think "what's next?". Quick check: nothing in today's topic is confidential.
2. **Everything you'll show is open and at its starting point**: the app or canvas ready, the tabs you need open, everything else closed. Notifications off. No private mail, chats or windows that could flash on screen. **API keys and tokens out of sight.**
3. **Dry-run anything fragile once**, so you know it works on camera. If a step still breaks mid-recording, say "cut" and redo just that beat (item 9).
4. **Recorder:** screen + webcam + mic on separate tracks; **system sound OFF** (mic only).
5. **Camera exposure and white balance MANUAL/locked.** Auto drifts and makes brightness and colour jump at cuts. Same framing and distance for every beat.
6. **Mic check:** levels good, room quiet enough.
7. **Green screen:** camera and seat in their marked spots; glance at the preview to confirm no room shows at the edges (see `recording-setup.md`).
8. **One recording per beat.** Leave 1–2 s of silence between beats so there's a clean edge to cut on.
9. **"Cut" protocol:** flub → pause, restart the sentence, keep rolling. The edit keeps the best take.
10. **Timer ≤ 45 min** for the whole session · water within reach.

---
**Export** (after recording, per beat): use **GPU encoding** if your recorder offers it. In the original setup, CPU export was about 5× slower (≈ 10 min vs ≈ 2 min). If the edit needs it, export screen and webcam as separate files. Name them in order: `01-…`, `02-…`.
