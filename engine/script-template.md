# SCRIPT TEMPLATE

**Rules:**
- **Match last week's script exactly**: same header, same beat headings, same blocks. A recognisable structure is what makes the edit fast and the coach useful week to week.
- **Director's commands** (`[SWITCH TO FULL WEBCAM]`, `[SCREEN: …]`) always go **on their own line, in italics, with a blank line before and after**. Never inline with the narration, and never ambiguous ("back to camera or hold the screen, your call" doesn't work; the edit needs a decision).
- **Walkthrough voice**: "here's the step that does X, and here's why it's set up this way." Never "now drag in a…"; the demo is already built.
- **Beat count drives runtime**, far more than wording. To shorten a video, cut a beat, not words.
- **Never re-explain something another video already owns.** Compress it to one sentence and point to that video with a card.

```
# Video NN — <working title>   | Format: screen walkthrough / talking head / emergency | Target: N min
Topic: <one line>
Safe to show: YES (public/synthetic data only, nothing confidential)

## Hook — full-screen you, ≤ 30 s
*[SWITCH TO FULL WEBCAM]*
VO: the pain, then the promise. Why watch, in one line.

## BEAT 1 — <label>
*[SCREEN: what's shown]*
VO: ...

*[SWITCH TO FULL WEBCAM]*
VO: ...

## BEAT 2 … BEAT n   (aim for 4–8 teaching beats)

## Outro / call to action — full-screen you, ≤ 30 s
*[SWITCH TO FULL WEBCAM]*
VO: one-line recap · the subscribe ask as an honest trade · a comment prompt · coming up next
```

**Mid-video:** one short subscribe ask at a natural seam (roughly the middle).
